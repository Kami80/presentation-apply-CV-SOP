i

Germany & Europe: conventions are not universal rules

Follow the university's instructions, the application portal, or the advertised PhD vacancy first. A photograph, date of birth, marital status, and full home address are not universally required; include them only when specifically requested and appropriate. Use reverse chronological order, explain grading scales when needed (for example, German grade 1.3, where 1.0 is best), and tailor the selection to the discipline. This seminar keeps its sample to two A4 pages; some doctoral vacancies explicitly request a longer academic CV.

Workshop principle

Do not just tell the committee that you are passionate or capable. Show what led you to the field, what you did, what you learned, and why this specific next step follows logically.

Beyond the reference letter

A practical SOP blueprint

These are useful narrative functions, not mandatory headings or a universal paragraph count. Follow the programme’s prompt, word limit, and disciplinary conventions first.

01

Opening: intellectual motivation

Start with a specific question, experience, or problem that led you toward this field. Avoid relying only on childhood ambition or generic passion.

Master’s + PhD
02

Academic preparation

Select relevant courses, concepts, grades where useful, and projects. Explain how they prepared you for the next level rather than listing a transcript.

Master’s + PhD
03

Evidence of ability

Describe one or two projects with the question or task, your own role, methods, outcome, and what you learned. Be precise about your contribution.

Master’s + PhD
04

Why this programme

Name relevant modules, research groups, facilities, approaches, or faculty only when verified. Connect each detail to a need or interest in your own preparation.

Master’s + PhD
05

Research fit & future direction

For a PhD, connect your research experience and methods to the advertised project or group. For a master’s, show what you want to learn and why now.

Emphasis differs
06

Closing: readiness & next step

End by bringing preparation, programme fit, and a credible next step together. Do not introduce a new life story or repeat the opening word for word.

Master’s + PhD
MASTER’S

Readiness to learn

Emphasize academic foundations, relevant projects, why this curriculum fills a real learning need, and how the degree supports a plausible next step.

PhD

Readiness to investigate

Emphasize research questions, methods, independent contribution, outputs where applicable, fit with the specific group or project, and a credible research direction.

Keep it honest: never invent results, publications, supervisor contact, programme details, or responsibilities. Do not force a hardship story into the narrative. Explain a grade dip or gap only when it is relevant, concise, and useful to the reader.
